How these figures are calculated

  • Every metric derives from one series of net monthly returns, compounded from the starting balance.
  • Drawdowns use month-end balances, so a deeper dip inside a month does not appear.
  • Sharpe ratio is annualised from monthly returns with a 0% risk-free rate.
  • Calmar ratio uses the full period, not the trailing 36 months some sources use.

Reading past results

Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. A long record shows how a system behaved in the markets it has seen; it cannot tell you how it will behave in markets it has not. Size any allocation for the drawdown, not for the average.
