How it works

Four stages, no discretion between them.

Nothing in the chain waits for a human opinion. That is the point — the same input produces the same trade every time.

01

Data

Years of XAU/USD price history, cleaned, aligned to session times and labelled for what happened after each bar.

02

Model

Models are trained to score the conditions that precede a directional move, and are validated on periods they never saw in training.

03

Risk

A separate rule layer sets stop, target and position size. It can veto a signal the model likes; the model cannot override it.

04

Execution

The robot places the order, manages it to target or stop, and writes the result to the log. No manual intervention.
