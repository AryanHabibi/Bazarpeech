The next layer In development

BazarPeech understands price.
Next, it will understand why.

We’re developing a fundamental-intelligence layer that uses machine-learning models and LLMs to read, analyse and interpret market news and information. It is intended to add external context to the decision engine — informing whether to open a new position, skip an otherwise valid setup, or adjust an existing one.
